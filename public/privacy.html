Privacy Policy

Launch draft — this document must be reviewed by privacy counsel and aligned with the actual hosting, email, analytics, search and AI providers before public launch.

Eligibility (18+)

eduRoll is temporarily restricted to adults aged 18 and over. Users are asked to self-confirm this when creating an account and signing in. This is a self-declaration and is not independent identity or age verification. Do not use the service if you are under 18.

Information we collect

Account information such as your name and email address; password hashes; password-recovery records; learning journey content and progress associated with your account; and limited technical/security logs needed to operate and protect the service.

How we use information

We use account data to authenticate you, protect your account, save and restore learning progress, provide support, prevent abuse, and operate the service. Search prompts and relevant learning context may be sent to configured search and AI providers to generate source-grounded educational explanations. Do not include names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise locations, health information, or other personal or sensitive information in prompts.

Third-party processing

Before launch, identify every provider and explain its role, region, retention, international transfer safeguards, and applicable terms. Do not send passwords, sensitive personal data, or confidential information to search or AI providers. eduRoll currently targets adults only. Standard OpenAI API data controls apply; eduRoll does not claim to have Zero Data Retention approval. The production provider configuration and data-use terms must be reviewed before launch.

Retention and deletion

Account and learning data are retained while your account remains active unless a shorter period is required. You can export your account data or request deletion using the account controls. Backups and security logs may persist for a limited period according to the final retention schedule.

Security

We use server-side password hashing, secure session cookies, CSRF protection, input validation, rate limits, and transport security. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and contact

Your privacy rights depend on your location. Before launch, provide the legal entity, privacy contact, data-protection contact where required, and a process for access, correction, export, deletion, objection and complaint requests.
