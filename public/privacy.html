Privacy Policy

Launch draft — this document must be reviewed by privacy counsel and aligned with the actual hosting, email, analytics, search and AI providers before public launch.

Information we collect

Account information such as your name and email address; password hashes; email-verification and recovery records; learning journey content and progress associated with your account; and limited technical/security logs needed to operate and protect the service.

How we use information

We use account data to authenticate you, protect your account, save and restore learning progress, provide support, prevent abuse, and operate the service. Search prompts and relevant learning context may be sent to configured search and AI providers to generate source-grounded educational explanations.

Third-party processing

Before launch, identify every provider and explain its role, region, retention, international transfer safeguards, and applicable terms. Do not send passwords, sensitive personal data, or confidential information to search or AI providers. The production provider configuration must be reviewed for age restrictions and data-use terms.

Retention and deletion

Account and learning data are retained while your account remains active unless a shorter period is required. You can export your account data or request deletion using the account controls. Backups and security logs may persist for a limited period according to the final retention schedule.

Security

We use server-side password hashing, secure session cookies, CSRF protection, input validation, rate limits, and transport security. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and contact

Your privacy rights depend on your location. Before launch, provide the legal entity, privacy contact, data-protection contact where required, and a process for access, correction, export, deletion, objection and complaint requests.
